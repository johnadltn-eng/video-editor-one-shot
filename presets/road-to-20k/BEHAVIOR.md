# Portable full-editor behavior

Reviewed behavior notes for the series preset and the local tools that were not in the pinned git commit. This is not a runnable skill and not a Mac or Premiere certification. The series workflow stays optional. Personal runbooks, client media, and machine paths are not part of this file. Numbers below are the reviewed behavior; helpers still refuse to change a live project or move an original unless the command explicitly says so.

## Creative preset and intake
- Retain `/20k` and the Road-to-20K visual preset as an available series workflow. Do not force its branding, series numbering, story or preferences on other projects.
- Project creation/intake needs a user-selected source folder and job, separated raw, broll/processed, premiere, hf-graphics/renders, transcript, outputs and contact folders. Copy intake media; never relocate original library footage. Probe rotation, nominal/average FPS and creation-time offsets, view contact sheets, fit stills/landscape footage over a blurred fill rather than crop important content.
- The existing personal runbook inferred the next day from earlier local jobs and used the last spoken next-day CTA to resolve conflicting numbers. Preserve the OPTION for series workflows, but parameterize series identity, day and prior-job context; stop for an unresolved identity conflict. Do not scan arbitrary Downloads or expose another user's project history. Never ship actual prior jobs or local location defaults.
- Strong hook first; problem/work/lesson/close, using the creator's own final CTA take. Keep any stated goal only if actually recorded and relevant. Repeated lines favor the last intended take; remove filler/dead air with measured word boundaries, not arbitrary timestamp guesses. Make profanity policy a preset choice, not a universal rule.
- Hold a reveal 1–1.5 seconds after it is named/pointed at. Ambient/no-dialogue shots use the best 1–3 second beats. Avoid padding and slivers under 6 frames.
- Story-driven voiceover b-roll: A1 talk continues; V2 shows the relevant thing. Keep face for hook, key claims, sensitive moments and CTA. Reuse prior footage only if authorized and copied into the job. Never put private screens/messages/client identifiers or spoilers on screen; request review/blur as needed. Sensitive material stays plain and is flagged for review rather than amplified with effects.

## Cut, timeline and revision contract
- Author phrase cuts from first AND last kept word START anchors; inspect RMS/slice-ASR across the assembled audio for hidden fillers, wrong word times and clipped syllables. Preserve original transcript timing when correcting aliases/words. Only scoped copies are edited.
- Canonical setup/bridge/export already match the Mac base. Fresh setup/replay is destructive to the target sequence: only on a dedicated fresh build or explicitly approved full rebuild.
- Inspect every track and snapshot the project/placement plan before follow-ups after manual edits. Preserve manual trims/deletions/additions and unrelated music. Never use TrackItem.move() to reorder; the local docs report phantom gaps/out-of-order track corruption.
- After every timeline change dump the REAL timeline; regenerate mapped captions/graphics and word times from actual clip source positions. Re-hook/reorder requires planned reconstruction and refreshed overlays, audio, punches and grade, not blindly replacing the hand-edited timeline.
- place.py clears V2–V5 and V1 items after the raw A-roll. Mirror the desired current state in place.json before running it. Track mapping: V1 picture, V2 b-roll/effects, V3 captions, V4 title/pops, V5 timestamps.
- Preserve the local place.py correction: effect renders under /fx/outputs/ carry base-cut audio; include them in linked A2+ removal. Remove only audio associated with placed media, never an unrelated music bed.
- Multiple projects may be open: activate the intended project by path; confirm ACTIVE sequence before audio-polish/joint-fades. Inspection itself can activate a project. A bridge timeout is a stop/recovery/verification condition, not permission to mutate another active timeline.

## Typography, card, captions and graphics
- Vertical 1080x1920 exact 30fps, upright, key visuals within x92–988/y163–1670. Respect right rail and bottom overlays.
- DAY N behind-subject cover card: Instrument Serif Regular white title fitted by builder to safe width; TikTok Sans Bold 40 kicker above it; no pill. Settle 115→100% over frames 0–8, drift to 103%; kicker fades frames 3–8. Shot-specific title height, body/title overlap >=10%, no glyph more than half covered; close selfie may need --top 175..220 and visual inspection. Optional next-issue card only when chosen, not a mandatory frozen end card.
- Preserve build_daycard.py cleaned STATIC median matte: exclude whole-frame/outlier coverage (primary 0.25–0.80; fallback 0.15–0.90; all only if no valid samples), median valid masks, Gaussian blur 2.0. This is the actual local DAY-card flicker fix. It avoids unstable per-frame and rolling-median alpha. It trades precise moving-subject occlusion for stability and needs per-shot review.
- Card output: versioned ProRes4444 alpha at 30fps, exact shot frames, placement on V4, preview strip. Frame 0 title opacity is intentionally zero; title fades over 6 frames, kicker begins at frame 3. Do not mistake intentional fade for a missing-card frame, or claim visibility on frame 0 without redesign approval.
- Captions across entire reel: TikTok Sans Bold 58, white, 4px black outline, no box; <=4 words/24 characters per line; phrase width prelaid and words pop at their beat. Break at punctuation, >0.35s pauses and picture changes; no single-word orphans/lowercase sentence starts. Keyword capitalization. Per-shot band check: nominal y998 face high/wide, y1340 b-roll/under chin, y1500 close chin-low selfie; adjust to actual framing/effects.
- B-roll timestamps only: Bold44, 4px outline, x100/baseline1600, up to 2s or shot length. Honor source timezone. Keep chronological order; omit backwards or reused-prior-day stamps.
- Text pops: 60px line/112px number, top y300, white bold/outline; 5-frame scale 0.70→0.88→1.06→1.02→1.00, hold then hard out. No added boxes/lower thirds/shadows by default.
- Keep live close for seamless loop rather than a frozen end card. Preserve any user-approved exception explicitly.

## Audio, grade and media corrections
- First audio-polish applies Amplify + Hard Limiter -2dBFS to A1 with --gain 0; THEN level_match.py targets each kept-speech source to -15LUFS, clamped -12..+15dB, with deliberate overrides for quiet/noisy sources. Applying polish again overwrites per-source gain. Verify with the same applied gain.
- 2-frame Constant Gain centered at each A1 joint; longer fades can reintroduce cut words. Optional light afftdn=nr=8:nf=-50:tn=1 is a derived copy only. Music is opt-in/user-added, preserved on A2.
- Final export target -14LUFS integrated ±0.5, true peak below -1dBTP; H.264 1080x1920 exact30fps 50Mbps. Existing export tool refuses out-of-spec results; investigate/measure the mix rather than promise normalization will always work. One local remedy was raising source gain 3dB, but this is not safe as an unconditional retry loop.
- Preserve warm look capability: Autumn-Rec709, Look Intensity70, Creative Saturation115 on V1/V2 only, never titles/captions/timestamps. This names existing behavior, NOT a license grant. Captured LUT/params are not bundled.
- Local grade.py 25.6.2 route adds per-footage Lumetri instead of adjustment layer; backup once, close target project, apply captured parameters to ALL Lumetri instances including self-closing BinaryHash references, reject dangling pointers, reopen. Reapply after re-placement. Observed closeDocument bridge loss makes safe closed-project workflow/recovery a material engineering gate; do not certify unattended safety from this source copy.
- media_fix.py blur produces a lower-frame feathered blur; cfr makes exact30fps for VFR lip-sync material. Both preserve audio and add transcript aliases. ACTUAL code then MOVES the job-local original into raw-unblurred/raw-vfr. Preserve the capability but explicitly review mutation semantics/error handling. Do not run it on original user libraries.
- Punch-ins optional: face-centered static105–110%, slow100→106%, five-frame emphasis punch capped112%; skip hook/card, V2-covered picture and sensitive lines. YuNet median of three samples/clip; save face points; reapply after rebuild. Model absent/rights unresolved is a visible blocker, not silent omission.

## Effects parity
- Preserve all 17 recipes, not only the basic four: orbit, couch wall, UI world, frame break, digital zoom, phone mockup, checklist, headline wall, zoom-through cut, chart grow, pull-back reveal, green screen, paper cut, vertigo, cover story, glass type, doodle boil. Vertigo stays available but OFF unless explicitly requested in this preset.
- fx_segments.py extracts frame-exact 1080x1920/30fps stretches from flat cut; optional hyperframes@0.8.16 u2net person mattes, :nomatte for effects not requiring them. Zoom-through can need a deliberate stitched landing shot.
- Copy helpers to projects/<job>/fx/build/. Preserve retiming/orchestration examples A/B, but author SEG, word beats, labels and user-supplied sample assets for actual footage. Existing numeric timings are illustrative, not an included client transcript. Rendered phone/UI windows are MOTION-GRAPHIC effects, never evidence of a real editor interface.
- White Inter Black, yellow #FFD84D keywords, dark translucent cards, Instrument Serif accents. No numbered effect labels/in-effect word captions for reels (NOLABEL=1); V3 captions persist. Shift caption band around frame break/orbit/pull-back when required.
- Preserve voiceover b-roll: alternate effects and relevant shots, split effect segments with source-in offsets instead of dropping b-roll. Keep any manual deletion of an unwanted effect deleted.
- Cards/tiles avoid faces, orbit at chest height, frame-break card lower when needed; transform matte with BORDER_CONSTANT for the corrected frame break to avoid mirrored ghost limbs. Graphic on the indicated side; two-word emphasis may fire on the second word.

## Approval and verification
- Default review gate before export; export only when requested. Posting is a separate action and not implemented by this bundle.
- Check real program frames, actual track completeness, linked-audio cleanup, captions/face/safe-zone timing, private material, color and final export loudness/dimensions. For parity testing later use the SAME rights-cleared footage and prompt on isolated projects; compare capabilities, story/cut intent, frame-level title continuity, manual-edit preservation, typography, grade, lip-sync, audio and export specs. Creative outputs need not be deterministic.
- No setup, editing, rendering or end-to-end verification was performed to create this review bundle.
